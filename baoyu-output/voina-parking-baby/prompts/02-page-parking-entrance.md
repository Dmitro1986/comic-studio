# Page 02: Киев. Паркинг. Вход.

## Visual Style
Art: realistic | Tone: dramatic | Layout: cinematic two-panel vertical split

## Generation Prompt

S.T.A.L.K.E.R. visual style. Vertical 2:3 page split into two panels by a thin black line. Top panel: the entrance to an underground parking garage in Kyiv. Brutalist concrete walls, rusted metal door frame, graffiti partially visible. A fluorescent lamp on a wire, half of it dead, the other half flickering with sickly yellow-green light. In the doorway, the silhouette of a young woman holding a tightly-wrapped newborn against her chest, looking inward at the dim light. The dark Kyiv night sky visible behind her, no stars. Bottom panel: extreme close-up of the newborn's face. Eyes closed, lips slightly parted, wearing a small red knit cap. The flickering lamp light falls from the side, making the face look almost waxen. In the shallow background, out of focus, the mother's silhouette looking not at the camera but into the dark stairwell. Photorealistic textures: concrete porosity, rust on the door, fabric weave of the cap, fine hair on the baby's face. Small handwritten Cyrillic text at bottom: "Сьогодні — п'ята ніч." Color palette: rust, ochre, dirty concrete, the red cap as only saturated color. No explosions, no violence, no weapons. Mood: descent into shelter, weight of protection. Aspect ratio 2:3 portrait.
