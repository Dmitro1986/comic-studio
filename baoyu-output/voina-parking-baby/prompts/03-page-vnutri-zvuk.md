# Page 03: Внутри. Звук.

## Visual Style
Art: realistic | Tone: dramatic | Layout: cinematic 4-panel grid

## Generation Prompt

S.T.A.L.K.E.R. visual style. Vertical 2:3 page divided into four square panels (2x2 grid). Each panel is a film still. Top-left: a young mother sitting on the concrete floor of an underground parking, back against a pillar, legs wrapped around a bundled newborn. Around her in the darkness, silhouettes of other people sleeping on mattresses, blankets, personal belongings scattered. Top-right: extreme close-up of the newborn's face, eyes now OPEN for the first time in the sequence — large, wet, unseeing, but reacting. Single light source from above through a ventilation grate, casting a striped pattern across the face. Bottom-left: the fluorescent lamp swinging slightly from vibration, the shadow of exposed rebar on the wall stretching and trembling. Bottom-right: the mother pressing the baby against her chest, her hand cradling the small head, eyes open, looking not at the camera but at something beyond. The baby's eyes are CLOSED again — protective reflex. Large yellow graffiti-style Cyrillic sound effects overlaid like tags: "ТИШИНА" (silence) top-left panel, "ГУЛ" (hum) top-right, "БАМ" (boom) bottom-left and bottom-right panels (twice). Photorealistic textures throughout. Color palette: rust, ochre, sickly yellow lamp light, concrete grey. No gore, no explosions shown — only the aftermath through vibration and light. Mood: the moment adult numbness and infant sensitivity diverge. Aspect ratio 2:3 portrait.
