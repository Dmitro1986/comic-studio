The contrast between warm interior (life) and cold exterior (the world waiting) is the emotional core. No text in the image except subtle bottom-right title: "День перший. Світло." in handwritten Cyrillic. Color palette: cold blue-grey for exterior, warm amber for interior, single white-yellow point of light. Grain, soft vignette. Mood: tender, fragile, the first moment of life.
