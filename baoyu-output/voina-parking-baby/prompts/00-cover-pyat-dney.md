# Page 00: Обложка — Пять дней

## Visual Style
Art: realistic | Tone: dramatic | Layout: cinematic cover

## Generation Prompt

S.T.A.L.K.E.R. visual style. Vertical 2:3 cinematic composition. Dark concrete ceiling of underground parking, water stains, exposed rusty rebar. Single sickly yellow lamp on a wire, casting dim cone of light. In the center of the frame, emerging from a white medical blanket — a tiny newborn hand, fingers reaching upward, impossibly small against the brutal concrete surroundings. Skin pale, slightly translucent, veins visible. The red knit cap on the baby's head is the only saturated color in the entire image — everything else is desaturated ochre, rust, dirty concrete grey. The lamp's shadow on the wall behind creates an unintended silhouette of an adult hand cradling the child. Photorealistic textures: scratches on metal, dust particles in the light beam, moisture on concrete. Cinematic depth of field, hand-held camera feel. Color palette restricted to: rust brown #8B4513, ochre #CC7722, dirty yellow #C8B560, concrete grey #696969, the red cap as only accent. Grain, vignette, no gore, no blood, no explosions. Mood: solemn, quiet, newborn fragility against brutalist backdrop. Aspect ratio 2:3 portrait.
