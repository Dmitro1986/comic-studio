# Page 04: Утро. Выход.

## Visual Style
Art: realistic | Tone: dramatic | Layout: cinematic horizontal top + square bottom

## Generation Prompt

S.T.A.L.K.E.R. visual style. Vertical 2:3 page with two panels. Top panel: full-width cinematic wide shot. Dawn breaking over Kyiv, grey-blue sky transitioning to pale gold at the horizon. The exit of an underground parking in the foreground — concrete ramp, the doorway framing the mother walking out into morning light, holding the bundled newborn close. Around the parking entrance: traces of the night — cold fire pit remains, abandoned water bottles, rolled blankets, a child's toy left behind. Cold mist near the ground. Bottom panel: tight close-up of the baby's face, eyes OPEN, looking directly at the camera/viewer. Morning sunlight falls from the side, no longer the sickly fluorescent but real warm sunlight. The mother is in soft focus in the background, out of focus, indistinct. Handwritten Cyrillic text on the top panel: "Світанок." (Dawn). Handwritten Cyrillic on the bottom panel: "Дивиться." (Looking). The baby's gaze is the emotional climax of the comic — first contact with the world outside the parking, first silent witness. Photorealistic textures: dawn light on concrete, breath mist, the baby's translucent skin in morning sun, fabric textures. Color palette transitions: cool grey-blue top, warm golden tones in the close-up, the red cap remains the only fully saturated color. Mood: tentative emergence, fragile hope, the contrast between shelter and the day. Aspect ratio 2:3 portrait.
